About Us

Real estate development, grounded in local knowledge.

Shuchana Development Limited plans and builds commercial and residential spaces across Dhaka, Mymensingh, Feni, and Faridpur. We work closely with each community we build in, so every project fits the place it stands in — not just the plan it started from.

Our Mission

Spaces people are proud to live and work in.

We believe good development is judged by how a space performs years after handover — not just on opening day. That means durable construction, sensible layouts, and a process that keeps clients informed from the first inquiry through to the final handover.

Leadership

The people behind Shuchana Development Limited.

Managing Director

Rafid Chowdhury

Rafid Chowdhury is the Managing Director of Shuchana Development Limited, leading the company's real estate development work across Dhaka, Mymensingh, Feni, and Faridpur. Under his leadership, Shuchana Development Limited has continued to grow its portfolio of residential and commercial projects, with a focus on responsible planning, durable construction, and spaces that hold up well beyond their handover date.

Rafid completed his education at the Institute of Business Administration (IBA), University of Dhaka. He brings that foundation in business and management to the daily running of the company, while staying closely involved in the planning and execution of every project Shuchana takes on.

In Memoriam

Remembering Architect Akhil Akhter Chowdhury

Shuchana Development Limited carries forward the legacy of Architect Akhil Akhter Chowdhury, a graduate of Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). He was the founding Managing Director of Suvastu Real Estate & Development Ltd, one of Bangladesh's established real estate companies, where his work in architecture and development helped shape residential and commercial spaces across Dhaka.

His dedication to thoughtful design and lasting construction continues to guide how Shuchana Development Limited builds today, carried forward in his memory by the next generation of the family.
